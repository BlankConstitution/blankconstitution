# BlankConstitution

*A blank page, and the nerve to write on it.*

We believe most people can be found within *Don Quixote*. The most important characters are **Sancho Panza**, **Don Quixote** and the literate duo: **the Priest and the Barber**.

**Sancho Panza** is how most people see themselves: rational, sound people, doing their best to reach a somewhat ambitious but realistic goal inside the terrible, crazy system someone else created. Seen from outside, though, **they are crazier than Don Quixote himself**. **They blindly accept a system created and imposed by others, lured by a goal that always seems just within reach.** They will never get there, but it feels close enough to keep going, or lasts long enough for them to start lying to themselves and get used to the crazy life they are living.

**The Priest and the Barber**, on the other hand, **can see themselves clearly and know what they truly want.** So the system imposed on them never swallows them up. They make use of it instead, have fun doing so, and often get what they were after. Free from other people's ideas and from their own lies, they stay truly rational: **they accept the system as it is and use its loopholes to get what they want.** Many times **they even take control of the system Don Quixote created.** From there they rule Quixotes and Sanchos alike. Sometimes they burn the books and lock Don Quixote's imagination behind a closed door. Sometimes they indulge his fantasy and play along, in a controlled, artificial way, never letting go of the reins.

Lastly, there is **Don Quixote**. We believe there have been very few in the history of humanity. Most were called madmen detached from reality, but a few, the lucky ones, changed the world. Don Quixote ignores the existing system entirely and creates his own, new and detached from everything before it. As he builds it, he creates his own values and goals too, and pushes forward towards them. Most Don Quixotes fail, rejected by the very system they ignored. They charge at giants that were only ever windmills, and the windmills stay where they are. But now and then one of them charges at something everyone else had agreed not to see, and the world, slowly and against its will, ends up turning his way. They were all called mad. **Only some of them were wrong.** Between the many who failed and the few who succeeded, the main difference is this: **the ones who were followed won.** Most times they were followed by Sancho Panzas, who simply swapped one system for another.

**What we want is for everyone to become Don Quixote**: an army of them, each pushing forward for their own system and ideals. Nobody is born Don Quixote. **Anybody can become one.** We want everyone to put their thinking into words, and say clearly what is wrong, what is right and what they want. These many systems will overlap, and the overlaps will give us a shared objective, however temporary. Once that shared version is built, the process goes on, each with their own again, keeping the same strong, individual and active spirit. Only like this can we truly change things together. Together, yes, but as unique individuals. **As Don Quixotes, better said.**

## What we hold

**1. Some things are not rights. They are facts.** Life, liberty, conscience, curiosity, imagination. Nobody gave them to you. You had them before there was a law, a State or a neighbour to owe you anything, the way you had lungs before you had a name. A right needs someone on the other side to respect it. These need no one, which is why **they cannot be taken away, only forgotten.** Every system that forgets them, that tries to rule as if people were not free by nature, ends up spending more and more to hold back what cannot be held, until it collapses or has to reinvent itself. We call them ***inherent faculties***. The fundamental rights in our constitution are born of them: **a constitution can only recognize them, never grant them.** Everything else we believe grows from here.

**2. So power is only ever lent.** If freedom is ours by nature, **whatever power the State holds, it borrowed from us.** And borrowed power left in one pair of hands forgets, very quickly, who it belongs to. **Splitting the work is not enough.** Almost every State today has a parliament, a government and courts, each with its own building and its own job. But when the government is chosen by the parliament's majority, and the judges are picked by both, the three answer to the same hand. That is one power wearing three uniforms: a **separation of functions**, a division of labour inside the same house. A **separation of powers** means separating their source. The power to make laws, the power to govern and the power to judge must each come from its own, separate election, so that none owes its seat to the others. Only then do they stand face to face, watch each other and have to bargain for the people who lent them their power. **A power that does not need the others will not listen to them, and it will not listen to us either.**

**3. And whoever holds it must answer to you by name.** **Separation means nothing if all three powers are handed out by the same party leaders.** **To be represented is to vote for a person**, someone you can look in the eye, call to account and send home. **Anyone may stand. You vote for candidates, never for lists**: single-member districts, or proportional systems built on candidates, like STV. Parties may exist as civil associations of people who agree with each other. **They do not own seats, and they do not live on everyone's money.** You are free to fund the ones you believe in. But a party paid by the State ends up sharing the State's interests, and stops answering to yours.

**4. Where this is missing, fear fills the gap.** A State that does not recognize your faculties has to keep you from using them, and **the cheapest way is to divide you.** Women's rights, property, immigration, healthcare, the planet: each of us gets sold a side and an enemy, and **in the fright we hand over our agency for a feeling of safety.** But **we are living beings. We evolved to meet chaos**, to adapt and survive against impossible odds, not to hide from it behind someone else's walls. Much of the frustration we all carry comes from being taught to reject the very chaos we were made for. Put sound institutions in place, and the same people who were set against each other can sit down and agree on what to do. **Most of them already want the same things.**

## Why we started

**We did not wait for permission, and we did not wait for a revolution.** If our faculties are already ours, **there is nothing to conquer. There is only the choice to use them.** So we did. We sat down, argued and wrote. **BlankConstitution is nothing more than a few friends exercising their inherent faculties in public, and asking you to do the same.**

If you have read *One Piece*, you know the family, and you know its tragedy. **Garp**, the grandfather, **puts his faith in the Marines and serves a State that promises justice and protection**, and he ends up watching a grandson die in its war. **Dragon**, the father, is the most wanted revolutionary in the world. And here is our crazy theory: **Dragon is the opposite of his son. He fights *for* liberty**, as something to be won some day, after the war, once the world has been brought down. **Luffy never fights for liberty. *HE IS FREE***, from the very first page, and he sets sail. He doesn't ask anyone to follow him. People follow him because he went first. And **those who join him are not Sanchos.** Every one of them comes aboard with a dream of their own, and next to him they find the nerve to chase it. **He doesn't make followers. He makes equals**: other Don Quixotes, each exercising their own faculties, sailing together because their kinds of madness happen to overlap, and **rejoicing in it**. He is Don Quixote, followed the way we hope to be.

**Don't fight for your freedom, and don't hand it over to be protected. Use it.** ***HOIST YOUR FLAGS!***

## What this is

A [skeleton constitution](constitution.md), short enough to fit on a page and open enough for anyone to build on. This text will never change. Fork it, and on your own branch write the constitution you would be willing to live under. Argue with ours, and with everyone else's. We start with Spain, Italy and the European Union, but this is meant for any people on Earth.

Never touched Git or GitHub? [Start here](docs/README.md). It takes ten minutes.
